Her story
Star Reading Jun is known as a Starseer — a rare existence capable of reading the language written in the stars. Where others see only light, she perceives something deeper: the color of a star, the rhythm of its pulse, the quiet distance between one light and another, the slow arc of its path through endless dark. From these, she draws meaning. Whether what she sees is truth, or simply what is meant to be — even she cannot say for certain. But those who have heard her readings tend not to forget them.
